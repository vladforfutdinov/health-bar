HealthBar

macOS menu bar status of the services you depend on. Free, open source, native.

One ring icon in the menu bar, split into a sector per service and coloured by its status. Click it: each service with its current state, the active incident, a 30-day uptime strip and the uptime of its least available component. Rows open the status page.

Install

brew install --cask vladforfutdinov/healthbar/healthbar

Or the DMG from GitHub Releases. macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with Developer ID and notarized. Updates install themselves.

Services

About 60 developer services are built in, grouped: Git and CI, AI, hosting and cloud, databases, packages, monitoring, auth, email and messaging, work tools. Any Atlassian Statuspage or incident.io page works by URL; the platform is detected automatically. A page of your own works if it serves the same JSON. The full list: docs/SERVICES.md.

Privacy

No account, no telemetry. The app talks only to the status pages you enabled and to its update feed. Privacy policy.
