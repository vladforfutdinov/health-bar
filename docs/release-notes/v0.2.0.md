## What's new

- **Menu bar icon**: a ring with one sector per service, so you can see which service is affected.
- **30-day uptime strips**: each service shows one bar per day and the uptime of its least available component. Hover a bar to see that day's affected components, their status and how long it lasted.
- **Live menu**: an open menu updates as each service answers. Refresh starts from scratch and keeps the menu open.
- **Light theme**: status colours with more contrast on light menus and menu bars.
- **Less traffic**: the status is read every minute; incidents and history at launch, on a status change, then hourly (every 15 minutes while a service is not operational).
- New app icon.
